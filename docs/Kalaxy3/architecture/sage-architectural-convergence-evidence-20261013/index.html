    SAGE architectural convergence — evidence checkpoint (2026-10-13)¶

    Status: Historical architectural/causal evidence for independent audit and reconciliation; not a newly authorized objective, replacement strategy, acceptance gate, or claim of completed deployment.

    Continuing Architect-owned objective: Restore functional SAGE role delegation and independent RACI, integrate qualified implementation, and resume the protected Architecture Helper workbench. The functional strategy and intent have not changed. This checkpoint records lessons from operating experience and clarifies the existing separation of responsibilities.

    Functional intent and delegation¶

    • The Architect uses ordinary language to explore objectives and decisions with a persistent LLM. That participant enriches functional understanding with innovation, alternatives, best practices, assumptions, risk and trade-offs; it does not inherit general workflow or implementation authority from being asked about an objective.
    • The Architect authorizes an objective-sized execution. Normal execution starts from a directly Architect-invoked fresh SAGE role; maintained, versioned repository-owned role context and relevant prior art are bound at invocation. The persistent LLM is not permitted to silently substitute a direct implementation or self-directed handoff. Bounded explicit Architect-authorized break-glass work is separately attributable and reconciled into evidence.
    • The fresh role uses SAGE governance and the established RACI. SAGE coordinates evolving tasks and operational state under the objective's existing authority. Routine implementation-local corrections do not create new objectives or approvals. New functional scope, significant trade-offs and actual changes of authority are returned for Architect judgment.
    • The Architect cares about coherent, transparent, traceable, efficient and cost-effective functional delivery, not the incidental internal mechanics of individual tasks or workflow states. Technical execution may be refused at its appropriate safety/authority boundary without rejecting the Architect's functional objective.
    • Referee, Reviewer, Critic, Narrators and other RACI participants provide distinct, independently attributable assessments. None acquires unilateral authority to reject or redefine functional intent. Disagreements, uncertainty and material options remain visible for Architect consideration.

    Evolving state and evidence¶

    • Objective-to-task management is an evolving state engine, not just a fixed dependency graph. Granularity, transitions, correction and recovery improve progressively using the established 80/20 principle and measurable experience. State is a projection of governed evidence, not a competing authoritative source.
    • SAGE is an open evidence system: legitimate outside intelligence, prior art, expertise and changing best practices can be injected with provenance, uncertainty and maturity. A candidate insight is not required to satisfy final acceptance criteria merely to be recorded and revisited.
    • Architecture Helper is the functional, stakeholder-facing view of SAGE: outcomes, evidence maturity, value, cost, uncertainty, trade-offs, alternatives, variation points, outstanding decisions, independent role contributions and truthful deployment status. Drill-down can show mechanics and receipts, but does not replace functional narration with task administration. Neither persistent-LLM reassurance nor narrator summary is authoritative over receipts.
    • Audit both directions: Architect decision → RACI contract → implementation → tests → observations; and existing rule → originating episode → evidence → applicable authority. An execution-instance file count, SHA, branch, specific path, or workaround is provenance unless evidence and authority justify generalizing it. Candidate contamination categories: durable invariant; objective-scoped requirement; execution-instance fact; revisitable implementation preference; unsupported restriction; contradicting control. Assess rather than pre-judge each case.

    Three separate Git/evidence responsibilities¶

    1. Git project chronology tracks how engineering work evolved (commits, branches, PRs, merges, milestones). It is distinct from the functional explanation of why work matters.
    2. SAGE functional/architectural evidence chronicles objectives, RACI contributions, decisions, alternatives, causal reasoning, assumptions, limitations, maturity, outcomes and lessons in repository-governed records, projected through MkDocs/GitHub Pages for independent inspection. Pages is a published projection, not a second authority.
    3. Delegated Git artifact lifecycle owns Git mechanics (branching, committing, integration and promotion) and produces receipts that SAGE references and validates. SAGE must not micromanage Git commands. Git identities and manifests bind particular artifacts; they are not fixed standing admission criteria.

    Kalaxy3 Git governed lineage remains the shared machine-neutral authoritative store for SAGE-owned causal facts; per-machine execution state is disposable. Externally owned proof remains authoritative in its own domain. Multi-user synchronization calls for early, frequent source-valid checkpoints with explicit maturity, not equating a commit with runtime qualification. Kalaxy3 deployments should converge with qualified SAGE repository state and expose desired-versus-observed version differences and runtime evidence.

    Observational evidence and limits¶

    • Repeated recovery and break-glass episodes exposed persistent-LLM execution substitution, speculative provider-call repairs instead of proven prior-art reuse, episodic details promoted toward governance gates, and objective-level progress displaced by internal readiness mechanics. The Architect reports approximately thirteen such episodes; that count is reported context, not an independently reconciled inventory.
    • Recent engineering-dispatch evidence recorded Referee allow and Reviewer reject against a narrow procedural assignment despite an unchanged functional objective. Several Reviewer allegations remained unverified; the episode did not demonstrate end-to-end functional delivery.
    • Front-door evidence showed fresh_role.invoked=true while HTTP 400 and workflow-manager-runtime-qualification prevented actual continuation. A break-glass local source correction later produced RECORDED CONTINUATION, with 57 tests passing, but a recorded handoff did not constitute executed fresh-role engineering or workbench restoration.
    • Earlier role evidence demonstrated independently invoked models and some role decisions; complete persistent/fresh handoff, authoritative objective-level integration and deployed workbench behavior remained unproven at this checkpoint.

    These observations warrant an independent role/RACI, architecture, evidence and contamination audit, without making that audit a new functional objective or a prerequisite to safe authorized progress. Preserve contrary evidence, evidence gaps, variation points and revisions. Reconcile current repository facts and live deployments before declaring any audit conclusion or closure.

    Audit/review and acceptance surfaces¶

    • RACI/authority: Confirm Architect-owned functional acceptance, separation of technical operation safeguards, actual accountable decision and correction inheritance.
    • Interface and context: Confirm explicit Architect objective authorization, fresh-role invocation, immutable original intent, maintained role prompts and evidence-backed prior art.
    • Evolving state: Confirm objective-sized continuity, task dependencies and revisitable 80/20 granularity without needless intervention.
    • Policy provenance: Identify episode-specific artifacts accidentally turned into universal rules; preserve legitimate hash/proof binding.
    • Human transparency: Independent Narrator/Referee/Reviewer/Critic perspectives, disagreements and relevant choices visible at the functional level through protected Architecture Helper and GitHub Pages.
    • Multi-user/deployment: Commit lineage and repository evidence reconstructable on another machine; Git delegated; deployment convergence and drift observable without claiming a source commit proves runtime behavior.

    Disposition: Chronicle this as existing-objective evidence. Audit findings should be separately attributed and reconciled with authoritative repository contracts; no rule, remediation, new approval, qualification or deployed outcome is conferred by this document alone.
